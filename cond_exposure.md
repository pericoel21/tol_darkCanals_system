## Enduring Exposure
A PC who has been exposed to extreme conditions for most ot the last day must pass an [[skill_endure|ENDURE]] [[coreMec_rollResolution|Roll]] or suffer a level of Exposure.
- The [[coreMec_difficulty|Difficulty]] of the [[coreMec_rollResolution|Roll]] is based on the severity of the conditions.
- The roll is usually performed at [[coreMec_watches|Daybreak]] of the following day, for environmental factors.
- The roll might be asked imediatly for special situations (like burning or a spell).
 
## Reducing Eposure
Each day, at Daybreak, Exposure is reduced by one if the PC is out of extreme conditions.
