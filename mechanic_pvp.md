When you directly oppose another player character’s action, success is determined a little differently. Both players roll, and compare their Success Dice (after removing any for Stress response). **If your highest Success die is higher than your opponent’s, you succeed and they fail,** then you check the other Success die for degrees of success. If your other die is also higher than their other die, you have succeeded with two degrees of success, if it is the same as their die, you have succeeded with one degree of success, and if it is lower, you are still considered to have succeeded (and by extension they have failed), but with 0 degrees of success. If both parties have the same result on their highest Success die, check the other Success die - if yours is higher you succeed with one degree of success, if both Success dice are the same, it’s a _true tie_ (a null result), and if it’s lower you have failed (with one degree of failure). **If your highest Success die is lower than their highest, you have failed** - if your other die is higher than their other die, you have failed with 0 degrees of failure, if it’s the same, 1 degree of failure and if it’s below, 2 degrees of failure. Only if you fail (not if it’s a true tie) do you gain Stress.
