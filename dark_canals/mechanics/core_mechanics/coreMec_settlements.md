## Safety & Shelter
The GM does not roll [[random wilderness encounters]] while on a Settlement.
> Doesn’t mean bad stuff isn't present in a settlement, just means it isn't randomly present.

It costs one [[coreMec_coin|COIN]] per week to stay in a Settlement for up to one week.

The weekly cost covers:
- Accomodations & stabling for horses.
- Meals (Helpful with [[stat_stamina|Stamina]]).
- Refills on some gear.
- Shelter (so PCs count as not Exposed in terms of the [[cond_exposure|Exposure Condition]].)

The weekly cost does not cover:
- Carousing.
- Buying new gear.
- [[item_ration|Food rations]].
- Repairs
- Medical treatment.
- Bribes or fines.
- Other [[coreMec_coin|non-Trivial payments & COIN expendature]].

## Greasing the wheels
For each [[coreMec_coin|COIN]] paid (in addition to any normal costs), the [[coreMec_difficulty|Difficulty]] of any [[skill_connections|CONNECTIONS]] [[coreMec_rollResolution|Roll]] in a Settlement becomes one step `Easier`.It cannot be any easier than `VERY EASY`.
