Only one [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] can be recovered per day.

## Carousing
A PC might recover one [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] by spending one [[coreMec_watches|Watch]] carousing in a settlement:
- Drinking, whoring, gambling...
- Hosting a [[funeral|Funeral]] for a fallen PC
- At a tavern or similar.

## Place of Natural Beauty
A PC might recover one [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] by spending one [[coreMec_watches|Watch]] resting or relaxing at a safe place of great natural beauty:
- Places of great natural beauty will be described as such by the GM
- They can only be used to recover Resolve once a year.
- They cannot be searched for unless you have been told of their existence by a local NPC, or perhaps found on a map.
- They can be discovered as part of exploration.
