# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

PCs can roll an additional die when performing [[dark_canals/mechanics/core_mechanics/coreMec_rollResolution|a Roll]] when:
- Performing an action they are Proficient at.
- Using an item they are Proficient with.

> So, the Modifier depends on "internal" factors to the PC.

Modifiers do not stack. The maximum amount of dice rolled as a modifier is 1.
