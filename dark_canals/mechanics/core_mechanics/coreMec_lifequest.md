Your character needs a strong core motivation that, most importantly, helps to guide your role-play. Your Lifequest can be pretty much anything, but there’s an art to picking a good one.

You want it to be something that’s achievable, but not so simple that you can get it done in short order; it has to be something your character will invest their time in, and drive them forward. When you are faced with tricky decisions in the game, you want to be able to look at your Lifequest and immediately know what your character would choose to do. It needs to be something individual to your character, specific to you, but perhaps broad enough to be achieved in
more than one way.

For example, regardless of your thoughts on organised religion in the real world, having that sort of conviction, something you believe in, is solid gold for an RPG character! Loyalty to an order of knights, or a king? Revenge? To regain your honour? Stuff like that.
