In performative or social situations it is best to roll before describing the action, and use the dice results to inform how you do so.
> It’s pretty silly to give a grand, inspirational speech, only to roll a failure - instead, roll the failure, then mumble your way through a horrible speech.

In-character speech is preferred where possible.
*Exception:* Situations using knowledge that a Character has, but a player doesn't (GM included).

> For example if your character is giving a lecture on a very specialized topic they know about.
