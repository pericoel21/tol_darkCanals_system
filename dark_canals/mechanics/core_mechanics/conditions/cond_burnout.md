## Effect
Burnout reduces a Character's [[attr_empathy|EMPATHY]] by one per level.

## Gaining Burnout
Once per day, when a PC hits zero [[stat_resolve|Resolve]], they gain a level of the Burnout [[conditions|Condition]].

## Recovering from Burnout
To recover a level of Burnout, a Character must expend one point of [[xp|XP]].

> Continually earning a single point of [[stat_resolve|Resolve]], then losing it, is a good way to lose [[attr_empathy|EMPATHY]] real quick. Since Resolve is harder to regain than Stamina, either be careful about spending it, or make sure you’ve got the [[xp|XP]] to deal with the Burnout.
