## Meaning of COIN
COIN represents a cost that is significant to the PC's life as an adventurer.

> "COIN" in this case isn’t necessarily a single gold piece. It might be referred to as "some COIN" or "a bag of COIN" or "just a few gold pieces" or some other indeterminate amount, but for the sake of the game, this all counts as 1 COIN.

## Cost Tiers
Every obtainable item has a Cost Tier:

| Tier         | Meaning                                                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Trivial      | If a PC is not [[coreMec_destitute\|destitute]], they can pay without tracking COIN. |
| Significant  | The PC tracks the transaction to adquire this good with COIN.                        |
| Unafforfable | Unnafordable for the PC's lifestyle or worth questing for.                           |
### Paying Trivial Costs
When adquiring Trivial goods or services, the PC does not track COIN.
> If you ask for something from some kind of shopkeep, publican, etc, and they say "Certainly, sir / ma'am", you can be assumed to have partened with a few coppers, but nothing is tracked.

### Paying Significant Costs
> Significant things might include buying a sword or a piece of armour, bribing the city watch, or living it up in a night of debauchery. 

The stuff listed in the gear section is all going to be Significant.
- Usually, each single item costs one COIN.
- Some, while costing one COIN, come as a kit that has multiple items.
- Other special items have Traits, and for each Trait it costs one COIN.

> If you ask for simething from a shopkeep and they say "Aye, let's see some coin", then the cost is one COIN.

#### Resale Value
Most of the items adquired throug COIN lose their value when used, so no Significant amount of COIN will be gained by re-selling them.

### (Not) Paying Unaffordable Costs
Some stuff is simply out of the reach of your standard adventurer.
> You will not be able to buy a keep, you will not be able to afford Artefacts like the Legendary Sword of Flensing Tempered in the Blood of Dragons, and you almost certainly won’t be able to afford to settle down and live a simple life.

This doesn’t mean these things are completely off the table.
- PCs won't ever adquire them through COIN.
- There might be something so valuable to someone, that they’re willing to part with the thing you desire in exchange for it.
- Unaffordable items might be worth questing for.
