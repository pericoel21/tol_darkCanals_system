# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

Sometimes, due to Injury or Conditions, your Attributes can be decreased from their starting
level, this is called Attribute Damage. Having decreased Attribute scores is going make you
worse at stuff, and in a fight, we sometimes call this a death spiral, but in Dark Canals, so long
as you have some Stamina or Resolve left you have some options about when that downward
spiral starts to affect you.
