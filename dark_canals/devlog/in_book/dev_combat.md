NATURALLY VIOLENT
What we’ve done in Dark Canals is tried to
take the natural way someone might de-
scribe acting in a violent encounter (‘I dodge
to the side, and swing for his head with my
axe!’) and give you tools that can turn that
sentence into mechanics, rather than look at
a list of combat manoeuvres and try to pick
the optimal one. That’s the way the game is
designed to be played - ‘fiction first’ - but fic-
tion first doesn’t mean you get to ignore the
mechanics if they won’t make a ‘good story’
or be ‘narratively satisfying.’ It just means,
you start with thinking about what you’d do
as your character, in the fiction, then use the
rules to mechanise those actions, then reify
any results. So learn the rules! But don’t
worry about them. You’ll be fine if you just
role-play

Alt turn orders
There are other ways of managing turn
order and initiative, but the alternating
PC turn/NPC turn works best with the
Reactions and Counteractions (pg84) me-
chanics, and with the Ranged Combat
(pg88) mechanics. A lot of what the alter-
natives do are accomplished by these me-
chanics, so check them out first!

Note that it’s also perfectly reasonable
that any PC or NPC might just do noth-
ing on their turn, or wait to act at crucial
moment, in which case they just wait for
their moment and jump in with their de-
scription of where they are and what
they do.
