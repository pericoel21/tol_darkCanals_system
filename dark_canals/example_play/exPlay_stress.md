For example, if you rolled a 1 on a Stress die, and the rest of your dice were a 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6,
normally your Success Dice would be the 5 and the 6, but because of the Stress, you ignore the
6, using the 4 and 5 as your Success Dice. If the Difficulty of the roll was AVERAGE or HARD,
that Stress die would turn this success into a failure!
