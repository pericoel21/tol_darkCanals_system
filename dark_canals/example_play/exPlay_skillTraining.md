# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.
If you increase a Skill by spending XP, you must also reduce the maximum (‘blanking out’ the
highest empty boxes) of any other Skill, equal to the level of the Skill you increased to. For
example, if you increased your BALLISTICS to 3, you would have to blank out a total of 3 boxes
from any of the other Skills. Maybe you reduce your maximum potential REASON, APPEAL
and INSIGHT from 5 to 4.
