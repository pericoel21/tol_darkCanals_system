REACTION/COUNTERACTION EXAMPLE
A guy swings his axe at you. You say ‘I dodge to the side and swing for his head with my
sword’. You would roll either MOBILITY or FIGHT, whichever was lower. A full success would
result in you avoiding getting hit by the guy and hitting them with your sword, a partial suc-
cess would mean you both receive a glancing hit from each other, and a failure would result
in you getting hit by them and failing to execute your Riposte.
