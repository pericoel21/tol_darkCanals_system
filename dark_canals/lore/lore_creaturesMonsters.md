ENDEMIC LIFE
Endemic Life is a term we use to mean all the
plants and animals that are natural and na-
tive to the setting. They don’t have to be re-
alistic or mundane, like bears and dogs and
so on, they can be fantastical, but the impor-
tant thing is that they are part of the natural
order of the world to which they belong.
MONSTERS
Monsters on the other hand are unnatural -
corruptions of the natural order, things the
locals have never seen before, things of dubi-
ous origin and without precedent. Have they
come to this world from beyond the stars, or
from a neighbouring dimension? Are they
from the deep past, slumbering in the earth
for millennia? Are they mutants, or cor-
rupted by supernatural phenomena? Are
they the result of magic, or the interference
of malign gods? What’s important is that
they do not fit into the world’s concepts of
life and death and are not part of the natural
ecosystems of the world. You can’t look them
up in the library, people who go to investi-
gate them never come back. And ideally, the
players have never heard of them either.
