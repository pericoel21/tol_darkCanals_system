FANTASY HEARTBREAKER
Contrary to what you might think, that
doesn’t mean it’s some sort of erotic ro-
mance game, but rather Fantasy Heart-
breaker is a derogatory term for a game
that’s just Dungeons & Dragons (with some
tweaks) that gets lost in a saturated mar-
ket of similar RPGs. This version of Dark
Canals is explicitly meant to live in simi-
lar tropes and settings as Wizard’s of the
Coast’s incredibly popular game, but if
you didn’t skip the pages before this one,
you’ll have seen that it has a completely
different vision for what RPGs are.
This Dark Canals was made to develop
the core system, whilst allowing it to be
play-tested with all the content made for
the innumerable fantasy heartbreakers
out there, but there was some ulterior
motive here: the next Dark Canals game
(which will not be a fantasy heartbreaker
at all, but more on that later). (Probably
a lot later). (In a different book). Until
then, though...
YOU ARE A MISFIT...
...but prefer the term adventurer. Communi-
ties spring up out of a mutual need for re-
sources, like shelter and food, and people
bring their skills and natural talents together
to create this interdependent group of indi-
viduals. If you had fit in to a group like that,
you probably wouldn’t have any desire to go
adventuring, you’d be working each day, on
the farm or in a workshop or whatever and
building your community, making babies,
drinking at the tavern, that sort of thing. But
for whatever reason, you’re bad at that.
You’re antisocial, greedy, violent, stupid, un-
productive or lazy, and are no use to the peo-
ple around you. So you leave.
You will not bathe, you will be unkempt, you
will be poor most of the time, get some ill-
gotten gold, then splurge it all on wine and
women and then be poor again. You know
you’ll never be a prince or princess. You
know you’ll never find someone who loves
you for you, because let’s face it, there’s not
much there to love. So you take what you get
when you can get it, and hopefully, when
death comes, you’ll die quick.
DUNGEONS
You’ll illegally enter places, you’ll kill the
things that live there, you’ll loot the place,
and hopefully not die this time.
DRAGONS
Yeah, there are monsters in the world. Some
are irredeemably evil, some are just hungry,
some are just trying to survive, like you. It’s
generally not worth it to find out which one
is which until after they’re dead though.
SURVIVAL
Starvation, exposure, dehydration, sleep de-
privation, disease and death. Not necessarily
in that order. You’ll have to explore the wilds
because you aren’t really welcome in civilisa-
tion, and you wouldn’t be much good there
anyway. So off you go, go sleep in a ditch.
TREASURE
You’ll need gold, to fix your stuff, to buy food
and shelter, to spend on your vices so you
don’t go mad. Well, how does one come by
gold, when all one is good for is violence?
