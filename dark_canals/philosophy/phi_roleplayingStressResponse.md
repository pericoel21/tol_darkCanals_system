ROLE-PLAYING PANIC
It’s up to you to role-play the results of Pan-
icking because of Stress. Trust me when I say
this, but making sure your character suffers is
very good for them, well... not good, per se,
but rewarding, for the player at the very
least, and maybe even for the character if
things turn around for them. Either way, you
(presumably) know what it’s like to be a hu-
man, and we humans are always screwing
stuff up because of Stress. When you see
those 1’s come up on your Stress dice, it is a
signal to you that your character has had an

involuntary response of some kind, but
they’re your character, so you get to decide
how that manifests. Pick the worst possible
thing for the situation, that’s the best one.
GM LED PANIC
If you’re not ready for this somewhat
masochistic style of role-play, because your
ego is too tied up in the character to choose
negative consequences for them, then a)
grow up, and b) you could let the GM choose
for you. When you’re mature enough to do
it yourself, let them know.
