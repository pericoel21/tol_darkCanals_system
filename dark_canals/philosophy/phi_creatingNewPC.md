CREATING A NEW CHARACTER
If you can do this between sessions, or before
you start playing, great, if you’re in the mid-
dle of a session, just go somewhere quiet
with the rulebook, a pencil, a fresh character
sheet, a few dice and get on with it.
Ideally, if your new character can be directly
related to the old one, or one of their core
contacts, or one of the other character’s core
contacts, the easier it will be to seamlessly
slip back into the role-play, but really, they
can be whoever.
