The best rewards for good role-play are the consequences of your actions.
What gets rewarded gets repeated, right? Well, if you enjoy role-playing,
a world that responds to your character’s actions (with appropriate conse-
quences) allows you to do that! A system that gives you points that let you
get out of the consequences of your actions is CIRCUMVENTING your
choices - your role-play! Forget that!
