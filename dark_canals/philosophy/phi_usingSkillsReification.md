FIRST, WE REIFY
Each of the Skills has a name, but they aren’t
particularly medieval fantasy sounding names,
are they? That’s because you’re not supposed
to say them when you’re playing, rather, you
invoke them through natural language de-
scriptions of the things you do. You won’t
say, for example, ‘I use my SURGERY Skill to
stabilise the Critical Injury,’ you’ll say ‘I tear
off the hem of my tunic and press it into the
wound to halt the bleeding.’
It might take some practice to get the hang of
this, and it might require you to pay attention,
but hey, role-playing is a skill, you are al-
lowed to get better at it.
