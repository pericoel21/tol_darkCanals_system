FEAR EFFECTS
Just remember that whenever the players
have to roll, if they fail, they gain Stress
(pg24). Not only does this mean that failure is
never just failure, but it also means that the
effects of fear are already built into the sys-
tem. If you have a creature that strikes fear as
part of it’s attack (as many games do), you
could just say that ‘just the sight of this horri-
ble thing is a test of your INSIGHT.’ There
need be no other consequence for failing this
roll than their Stress increasing, which is
something they can role-play, and there you
go - you’ve simulated fear.
