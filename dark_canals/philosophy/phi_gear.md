+1 TEAPOT
Getting cool gear is cool, but if a character is
just their stuff, a) they’re boring, and b) they
cease to be who they are if they lose it, and
the player might not be able to even role-
play them anymore! So GMs pull their
punches to allow the characters to never be
without their stuff.
On the other hand, if a sword is merely a
sword, then a) what you do with it is what’s
important, and b) there’s plenty more fish in
the sea.
In Dark Canals, gear is crucial to success in
many situations, but not because ‘numbers
go brr,’ but because useful items are useful for
the thing they are useful for! The number of
times I’ve had to write a sentence as redun-
dant as that in this game is getting ridiculous.
DO CARS MAKE IT EASIER TO DRIVE?
Not really? Cars don’t make driving easier,
they make it possible. If you’ve got a +1 Car
when using your DRIVING Skill, what does it
look like when you use that Skill without a
car? It looks pretty dumb, that’s what.
Dark Canals doesn’t have cars (or a DRIVING
Skill) but it does have long bows and a BAL-
LISTICS Skill. Although it certainly is the case
that a good piece of gear can make some ac-
tivity easier than using a poor piece of gear,
it seems that user skill is the biggest salient
factor in success. Maybe you’ve seen one of
those videos where they give a cheap
espresso machine to a pro barista and a pro-
fessional machine to an amateur and see how
well they do. Would it surprise you to know
that the pro can do more with less than the
amateur with more? So yes, if you’re using an
item you’re Proficient with in Dark Canals,
you get a +1 Modifier, but it’s your Profi-
ciency that’s making the difference. Any
items that don’t require a Proficiency simply
make it possible to do the thing the item is
for. There might not be a technical Profi-
ciency to use crampons, but they’re sure use-
ful if you’re climbing an ice wall! And, it
might be HARD to do without them!
