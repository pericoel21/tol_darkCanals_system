O dice roll can ever stop a player (including the GM) from role-playing their charac-
ter. That means you can’t force anyone to do anything just because you rolled well.
That isn’t what the social Skills are for - they’re for informing role-play, not replacing
it.
SOCIAL SKILLS
RPGs are social games, you play them by talk-
ing. This gives in-character social interac-
tions a sort of special status, because in this
case what the player and what the character
are doing, is the same. The exact words might
be different, ‘translated’ from whatever lan-
guage the character uses, but ultimately, in-
character social interactions are just handled
by speaking in-character. PCs and NPCs can
talk to each other in as natural a conversation
as any other you might have at the table.
