# `= this.file.aliases[0]`

A found weapon (that was laying around for more than a day) will be rusted.

## Effects of Rust
- Counts as [[trait_damaged|Damaged]] for when performing a [[reactions_block|BLOCK]] [[combat_reactionsAndCounteractions|Reaction]].
- Item looses the beneficial parts of its [[mechanic_itemTraits|Traits]].
