## Using Pipeweed
- When using pipeweed, an unsafe location can be made to feel safe in terms of [[mechanic_recoveringStress|recovering Stress]].
- This counts as a night of heavy smoking.
- The somker's [[stat_stress|Stress]] is reduced to zero.
- A ration of pipeweed is consumed.
- The smoker suffers a level of [[condition_exhaustion|Exhaustion]].
