## Using Booze
- When using booze, an unsafe location can be made to feel safe in terms of [[mechanic_recoveringStress|recovering Stress]].
- This counts as a night of more or less heavy drinking.
- All [[stat_stress|Stress]] is reduced to zero by participants.
- A ration of booze is consumed per character participating in drinking.
- Participants suffer a level of [[condition_dehydration|Dehydration]].
