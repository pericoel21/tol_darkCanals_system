## Hiring Hirelings
Roll [[skill_connections|CONNECTIONS (EMPATHY)]] in a [[mechanic_settlements|Settlement]] to find Hirelings.
- [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] set by the size of the settlement.
- [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] modified by the reputation of the hiring party.

## Paying the Hireling
They want:
- 1 [[mechanic_coin|COIN]] in advance.
- 1 [[mechanic_coin|COIN]] per week of service (or part thereof, if dismissed early).
- 1 [[mechanic_coin|COIN]] per [[mechanic_injury|Injury]] of `SEVERE` tier or higher they sustain while on service.

## What will a Hireling do?
- Do what they are asked for within reason, and if it fits the contract they were hired for.
- Not put themselves into danger.
- Talk back at home, increase or decrease reputation, depending on pay, danger, harm...
