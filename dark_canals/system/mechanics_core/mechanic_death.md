## Ways to Die
A character dies if they...
- ...suffer a [[mechanic_injury#Lethal Injuries|Brutal Injury]] and fail an [[skill_endure|ENDURE]] [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] before being [[mechanic_injury#Lethal Injuries|Stabilized]].
- ...suffer a [[mechanic_injury#Brutal Injuries|Brutal Injury]] to the [[combat_hitLocations|Head or Torso]].
- ...mark out all the boxes of the [[condition_starvation|Starvation]], [[condition_dehydration|Dehydration]] or [[condition_exposure|Exposure]] [[mechanic_conditions|Conditions]].

## Retriving the Corpse
- PCs get one [[mechanic_coin|COIN]] by bringing the corpse to one of the [[mechanic_coreContact|Core Contacts]] of the late PC.
- Retrieving the corpse allows for a Funeral to happen.

## Weight of a Corpse
- The gear a dead character was carrying must be transferred from their inventory (or backpack) in order to transport it.
- The corpse itself counts as equipping two [[heavyITems|Heavy Items]] (akes two people to carry one corpse).
- While carrying a corpse, a PC cannot hold anything else with their hands (if they pick something up, they drop the corpse).
