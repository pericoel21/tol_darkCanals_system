To buy stuff, a PC must be in a [[mechanic_settlements|Settlement]] and spend at least one [[mechanic_watches|Watch]].

## Finding Goods & Services
As long as conditions are met, rolls for Shopping can be done withouth the GM's overview.

To find a merchant or a vendor while in a settlement, spend one [[mechanic_watches|Watch]] (must be morning or afternoon) and [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] [[skill_connections|CONNECTIONS]].

The [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] is determined by the size of the [[mechanic_settlements|Settlement]].

| Size          | Difficulty  |
| ------------- | ----------- |
| Tiny Hammlet  | `Very Hard` |
| Rural Village | `Hard`      |
| Town          | `Average`   |
| City          | `Easy`      |
| Capital City  | `Very Easy` |

If the PC succeeds on the [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]], they find a merchant who has any item associated with their trade, or a vendor who can perform their trade service.

If the PC fails, thet can try again in the next [[mechanic_watches|Watch]]that is not evening or night.

The [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] to find merchants must be done each time.
> The merchant you find could have been a travelling merchant, or the established ones may be shut, or out of stock.
