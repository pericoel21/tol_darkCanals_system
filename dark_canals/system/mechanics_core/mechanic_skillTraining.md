## Requirements
When a PC trains a [[mechanic_skills|Skills]], they must:
- Select a [[mechanic_skills|Skills]] to train that is not based on an [[mechanic_attributes|Attribute]] with [[mechanic_attributeDamage|Attribute Damage]]. 
- Spend a [[mechanic_watches|Watch]] training.
- Reduce their [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] by one.

### Tutors
Once a [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] reaches level 3, a tutor is needed to keep progressing.  Each session of training costs [[mechanic_coin|COIN]].

## Using XP from training
Training grants the PC one [[xp|XP]] in the selected [[mechanic_skills|Skill]]:
- Mark it with the first letter of the [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] in the next available XP box in the character sheet.
- If no box is available, the new letter replaces the oldest entry (even if it is gained by means different than Skill Training).
- Keep track of the oldest XP box with a paper clip or a mark.

### Leveling Up a Skill
Once the PC has 10 XP boxes filled for one specific [[mechanic_skills|Skill]], erase them and increase the [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] by one.

Then, reduce the maximum of a number of [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] equal to the level you reached for the trained [[mechanic_skills|Skill]].
- Blank out the boxes on the right to represent this.
- Reduce at most one box per skill, possibly in several skills.

> Training a Skill is a commitment, you can’t be good at everything!



GOLD FOR XP
If you’ll notice, earning XP costs Resolve, and earning Resolve costs COIN. Therefore, in a round-about kind of way, COIN is XP!

But only if you earn enough of it to cover the costs of your gear, provisions, medical care and so on, and still have some left over. So  if you want your character to improve their Skills and learn new Proficiencies, you will need to makes sure each dungeon delve is lucrative enough!

TOO MANY EGGS, ONLY ONE BASKET
There are a finite number of XP boxes on your character sheet. If you ever fill them all up, then the next time you earn an XP it replaces the oldest entry. There’s only so much stuff you can hold in your head at once, and unless you commit to something you might start forgetting things you’ve learned!
