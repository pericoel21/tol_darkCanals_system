# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

Artefacts cannot be bought but they can be fenced for at least 1 COIN when sold, and potentially more, depending on the buyer.

> [!tip] Example artefacts:
> A piece of fine jewellery, a sword with a name, the tapestry of a long dead weaver, or a strange statuette in the shape of a reptilian god that makes you feel strange when you look at it.

> [!tip] Find Artefacts...
> ...in dungeons, in dragons’ hoards and in the vaults of the wealthy and powerful.

## Fencing an Artefact
When in a settlement you can spend a [[coreMec_watches|Watch]] looking for a buyer for any Artefact in your possession.
