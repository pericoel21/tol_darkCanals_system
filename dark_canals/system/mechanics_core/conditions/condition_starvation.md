Every day, at [[mechanic_watches|Daybreak]], PCs suffer a point of Starvation. It does not matter how much the Character has eaten in the previous day.

For each [[item_ration|Ration of Food]] consumed, the Character reduces the Starvation [[mechanic_conditions|Condition]] by one. This is handled separately to any food consumed to recover [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] by [[mechanic_eating|Eating]].
