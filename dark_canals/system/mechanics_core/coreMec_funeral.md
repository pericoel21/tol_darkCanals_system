## Requirements
- Hosting a Funeral takes at least one [[coreMec_watches|Watch]].
- The corpse of the PC must be [[coreMec_death#Retriving the Corpse|Retrived]].

## Benefits
### Exceptions
The benefits of the Funeral do not apply to PCs who:
- Hated the deceased PC.
- Were responsible for their death through malicious intent or negligence.
- The deceased PC died from [[cond_dehydration|Dehydration]] due to [[item_booze|Booze]] consumption at the Funeral of another PC.

### XP
Every way to gain [[xp|XP]] from a funeral has a cap of five [[xp|XP]].

#### For Still Living PCs
Every still living PCs can spend [[coreMec_coin|COIN]] individually to gain one [[xp|XP]] per [[coreMec_coin|COIN]]. It counts as [[coreMec_fulfillingResponsibilities|Fulfilling Responsibilities]].

#### For the Replacement PC
The new PC for for the player whose old PC died can also gain [[xp|XP]].
- This PC must be present in the funeral, and be related to the deceased PC.
- The amount of [[xp|XP]] gained is equal to the combined amount of [[coreMec_coin|COIN]] the other PCs spent.
- It also counts as [[coreMec_fulfillingResponsibilities|Fulfilling Responsibilities]].

### Carousing
For each PC, individually spending one ration of [[item_booze|Booze]] in the Funeral makes it count as a [[carousing|Carousing]] [[coreMec_watches|Watch]].
