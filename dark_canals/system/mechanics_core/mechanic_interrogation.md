A character cannot make another character do something just with mechanics. It’s up to the player (or GM) who is role-playing them to decide what they do.

Interrogation rolls help inform role-play in a direction that allows a character to coherse or convince another one

## Pressing for Information
Pressing a character to give information about a topic which they are not initially willing to part with.

### Opposed Roll
Resolved through an [[mechanic_opposedRolls|Opposed Roll]]:

| Situation            | Active Skill                 | Opposed Skill              |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Intimidation         | [[skill_physique\|PHYSIQUE]] | [[skill_endure\|ENDURE]]   |
| Appealing to emotion | [[skill_appeal\|APPEAL]]     | [[skill_insight\|INSIGHT]] |
| Appealing to logic   | [[skill_reason\|REASON]]     | [[skill_reason\|REASON]]   |
### Success
If successful:
- The pressed character talks some more.
- Provides some new information.
- The information doesn't need to be true.

### Number of Attempts
#### Regular Number of Attempts
- A character can be pressed for information a number of times equal to their current [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] Resolve.
- At their limit, they are unwilling to talk any further.

#### Violent Interrogation
- If the character is held against their will, they can continue to be pressed for information past their [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] limit.
- Each additional press reduces their [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] by one.
- If their [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] hits zero, they pass out.


## Assessing Personality
Find information about a character's personality through as [[skill_insight|INSIGHT]] [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] (it is not [[mechanic_opposedRolls|Opposed]]).

On a success:
- Learn which of their social [[mechanic_skills|Skills]] (REASON, INSIGHT or ENDURE) is highest.
- If you succeed with at least one [[mechanic_degreesOfSuccess|Degree of Success]], the lowest social [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] also becomes known.

### Describing Characters Based on Social Skills
The highest and lowest social [[mechanic_skills|Skills]] can be used to describe a character's personality, and to roleplay accordingly.

| Skill                      | If Highest | If Lowest    |
| -------------------------- | ---------- | ------------ |
| [[skill_reason\|REASON]]   | Smart      | Unreasonable |
| [[skill_insight\|INSIGHT]] | Suspicious | Naive        |
| [[skill_endure\|ENDURE]]   | Confident  | Insecure     |
