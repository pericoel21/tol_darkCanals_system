## Uses of Stamina
- *Pushing:* [[attr_strength|STRENGHT]] and [[attr_agility|AGILITY]] [[coreMec_rollResolution|Rolls]] can be [[coreMec_pushingRolls|Pushed]] by spending one Stamina, ignoring [[coreMec_attributeDamage|Attribute Damage]].
- *Combat-specific:* Certain actions in [[combat|Combat]] require spending Stamina.

## Recovering Stamina
- [[coreMec_sleeping|Sleeping]] one Stamina per hour if the PC sleeps at least three hours.
- [[coreMec_eating|Eating]] recovers one Stamina per ration consumed.

## Stamina at PC creation
The starting & maximum Stamina for a PC equals the average of their [[attr_strength|STRENGTH]] and [[attr_agility|AGILITY]] scores, rounding up, plus one.

## Tracking Stamina in the Character Sheet
- Mark the maximum Stamina by separating the unavailable boxes with a line.
- Track the spent Stamina by marking boxes from the botom up.
