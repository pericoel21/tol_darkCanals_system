## Uses of Resolve
- *Pushing:* [[attribute_wits|WITS]] and [[attribute_empathy|EMPATHY]] [[mechanic_rollResolution|Rolls]] can be [[mechanic_pushingRolls|Pushed]] by spending one Resolve, ignoring [[mechanic_attributeDamage|Attribute Damage]].
- *Keep your Cool:* Spend an amount of Resolve to cancel the same amount of [[stat_stress|Stress Dice]] with an outcome of 1.
	This can completely cancel a [[stat_stress#Stress Response|Stress Response]].
- *Mind to Body:* Spend one Resolve to gain, and immediatly use, one [[stat_stamina|Stamina]]
- *Training:*  
- *Bonding:*

## Burning Out
Running out of Resolve causes a level of the [[condition_burnout|Burnout]] [[mechanic_conditions|Condition]].
![[condition_burnout#Effect|Burnout]]

## Recovering Resolve
Once per day, a PC might [[mechanic_recoverResolve|recover one Resolve]] by Carousing, or in special occasions, by visiting a place of great Natural Beauty.

## Resolve at PC creation
The starting and maximum Resolve for a PC equals the average of their [[attribute_wits|WITS]] and [[attribute_empathy|EMPATHY]] scores, rounding up, plus one.

## Tracking Resolve in the [[character_sheet|Character Sheet]]
- Mark the maximum Resolve by separating the unavailable boxes with a line.
- Track the spent Resolve by marking boxes from the botom up.
