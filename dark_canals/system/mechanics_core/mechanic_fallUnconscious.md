A player can choose to make their PC unconscious at any point if:
- The PC has suffered a [[mechanic_injury#Lethal Injuries|Lethal Injury]].
- Or, the PC has suffered [[mechanic_attributeDamage|Attribute Damage]] exceeds their maximum in that [[mechanic_attributes|Attribute]].

While Unconscious, [[skill_endure|ENDURE]] [[mechanic_rollResolution|Rolls]] to avoid [[mechanic_death|Death]] from a [[mechanic_injury|Lethal Injury]] Injury are at `EASY` [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]].
