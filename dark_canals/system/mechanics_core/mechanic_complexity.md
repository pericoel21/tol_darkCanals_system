## Complexity vs. Magic
When determining the [[magic_spellLevel|Spell Level]]:
- It influences [[technique_evocation|EVOCATION]] (creating something) & [[technique_alteration|ALTERATION]] (changing something).
- It does not influence [[technique_transformation|TRANSFORMATION]] (moving something).
