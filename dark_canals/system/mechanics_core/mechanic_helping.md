## Helping Before Rolling
One or more people can help with an action before rolling, lowering the [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] of the [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]].

For each helping Character:
- If the help is directly tied into the action, the [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] is reduced one step without additional rolling.

- If the help comes from a separate action to the main one, a [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] might be needed to lower the [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] one step.

Helping cannot reduce [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] lower than `Very Easy`.

## Helping After Rolling
If a roll fails by one [[mechanic_degreesOfSuccess|Degrees of Success]], it is possible to help as an action that happens immediately after the one which failed.
- The helping action must make sense and happen very close to the failed one.
- It might require a [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]].

In this case, a successful helping action transforms the previous [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] from a failure with -1 [[mechanic_degreesOfSuccess|Degrees of Success]] to a success with 0 [[mechanic_degreesOfSuccess|Degrees of Success]].
