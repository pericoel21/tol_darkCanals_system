To put on or take out a single piece of armour, a character must spend...
- 2 [[combat_turn|Combat Turns]] for all body [[combat_hitLocations|locations]] but `HEAD`.
- 1 [[combat_turn|Combat Turn]] for `HEAD` armour.

## Layering
- [[hub_armour_mail|Mail]] armor pieces can go over [[hub_armour_leather|leather]] ones.
- [[hub_armour_plate|Plate]] or [[hub_armour_reinforcedPlate|reinforced plate]] can go over [[hub_armour_mail|mail]].
- No more than one layer of a type can be equipped per location.
- Leather is needed under any metal layer. If not, the character suffers a [[mechanic_injury|Minor Injury]] to that [[combat_hitLocations|Hit Location]]. The injury goes away as soon as the armor is removed.
