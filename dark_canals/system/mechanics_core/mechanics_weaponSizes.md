# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

As size for the intended use increases, the weapon does too: [[trait_short|Short]] -> [[trait_oneHanded|One-Handed]] -> [[trait_heavy|Heavy]] or [[trait_thrown|Long]] -> Not usable (too big to wield)

> A One-handed weapon for a large creature is as big as a Heavy (or two-handed weapon) for a medium creature. A Heavy weapon for a large creature would be so big, no medium creature could wield it. A troll could use a human’s One-handed sword as a dagger! Likewise, a Short weapon (like a dagger) for a medium creature would be as big as a One-handed weapon for a small creature and so on.
