## Dying in a Dungeon
If your character dies in a dungeon somewhere, note the cause of death on your character sheet, then cut the inventory/backpack section off with scissors and hand it to the GM.
They’ll take a note of where all your stuff has been left, and if another player happens to
find it, they’ll be able to hand you the backpack, to take from it what was left behind.
