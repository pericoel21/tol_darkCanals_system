# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

During their turn, while in cover, a character might stay In Cover or pop out of Cover.

## Movement Actions

Movement (in this case) includes things like
fleeing, charging, raising/drawing a weapon,
or popping out of cover, but does not in-
clude someone firing a weapon that they al-
ready had ready or, say, slitting a hostage’s
throat with a knife they already had pressed
against it. If you try in these sorts of situa-
tions, both actions happen simultaneously.
As a general rule, it’s any movement that
changes the character’s bodily location, not
just the movements of their body.

> Things like fleeing, charging, raising/drawing a weapon, or popping out of cover. Not like someone firing a weapon that they already had ready or, say, slitting a hostage’s throat with a knife they already had pressed against it.
- Characters in Overwatch get to shoot first.

## Pop Out of Cover
- Counts as [[movement|Movement]] ([[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] for [[combat_ranged|shooting]] is `HARDER`).
- Characters in Overwatch get to shoot first.
- Whether Overwatch fire hits or misses, character might go back In Cover.
- If not back In Cover, character can [[combat_ranged|return fire]].
- Character can get back In Cover.

## Overwatch
- Character is Out of Cover.
- Character must have declared they're watching for someone or an area.
- Character gets to act first in reaction to others' [[movement|Movement]] (including poping out of cover).
