## Turn Order
- *PC turn:* All PCs act together
- *NPC turn:* All NPCs take a turn.
- *Initiative:* Whoever acts first, goes first.
- *Who Goes Next:* Keep it intuitive.
	- Usually, around the table, clockwise.
	- Sometimes a character goes because it makes sense.
	> Closest character, whoever is ready...
- *Multiple characters in one turn:* When a group outnumbers an enemy, they might attack together.
- *Out of Turn:* Characters can spend a [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] to act outside their turn.
	> See [[coreMec_combat_reactionsAndCounteractions|Reactions and Counteractions]]

## Action Economy
Each turn, a player answers the questions:
- *Where they are:* Moving, positioning or a short action.
	- Characters can run up to two [[coreMec_combat_distance|Zones]].
	- Getting up from [[prone|Prone]] also counts.
	> Think of short actions as setting up another action:
	> Drawing weapons, aiming, using items...

- *What they do:* Long Actions.
	- Anything that requires a [[coreMec_rollResolution|Skill Roll]].
	> Attacking, firing a weapon, reloading,

> *Where are you?* and *What do you do?* are guides to help players to describe functional turns.
>
> The objective is that they learn to roleplay turns that feel fair but cool.
>
> Don't let counting actions hurt roleplay.
