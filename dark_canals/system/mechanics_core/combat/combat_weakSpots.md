- To attack a weak spot, it must be declared beforehand.
- To hit a weak spot, the [[combat_hitLocations|Hit Location die]] must be a 6.
- When hit, the [[mechanic_injury|Injury]] is resolved for the general [[combat_hitLocations|Hit Location]] the weak spot is in, but an additional effect is added on top.
