# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

Turn portions of [[rawFood|Raw Meat]] into Leather [[coreMec_crafting|Scraps]].

Up to four [[rawFood|Raw Food]] portions can be tanned by a PC during one [[coreMec_watches|Watch]], at a [[coreMec_makingCamp|Camp]] (it needs to be already set up).

The PC rolls [[skill_techne|TECHNE]], at `VERY EASY` [[coreMec_difficulty|Difficulty]].

A Success allows one [[rawFood|Raw Food]] to be tanned, creating a piece of leather [[coreMec_crafting|Scrap]] The amount of leather doubles with each [[coreMec_degreesOfSuccess|Degree of Success]] (1, 2, 4, 8).

On a Fail, or if not enough [[coreMec_degreesOfSuccess|Degrees of Success]] are rolled, some portions of [[rawFood|Raw Food]] might not be coverted. All remaining [[rawFood|Raw Food]] is wasted.
