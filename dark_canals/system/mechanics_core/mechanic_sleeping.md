# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

Sleeping recovers one [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] per hour, if a PC sleeps three or more hours.

In addition, for every [[mechanic_watches|Watch (three hours)]], a PC recovers one level of [[condition_exhaustion|Exhaustion]].
