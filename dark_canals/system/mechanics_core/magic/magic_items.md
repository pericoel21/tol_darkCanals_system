## Lore
- [[magic_lattices|Lattices]] can actually be infused with magic ove long periods of time if the source is powerful enough, like a metal getting magnetized
> Something, something, all the "magic molecules" align themselves with chaos.

- [[magic_lattices|Lattices]] infused with magic can be worked to obtain magical items.
- The magic is constantly lost while working.
> A very good magic sword was probably created by a great smith, who was able to make each hit and each heat count, not doing a single operation in vane.
> A poor magical item was probably worked and reworked again, or made from second-hand material.

- Artisans able to work magic items are exceedengly rare or all dead.
- Techniques used to work magic items are lost to time or ferociously guarded.
