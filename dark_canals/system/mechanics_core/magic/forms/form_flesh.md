# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

Magical art of humanoid bodies. This art affects dead bodies and the bodies of non-human humanoids, as well as those of living humans.
Since natural philosophy asserts that these things have no more in common than their appearance, and FLESH does not affect human statues, Arcane theorists are puzzled by the range of this Form.
