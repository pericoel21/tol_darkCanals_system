# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

When [[magic_casting|Casting]], count every 1 rolled in [[stress die|Stress Dice]]. That determines the [[mechanic_intensity|Intensity]] of the Mishap (so, the column used in the random table).
- [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] can be spent in a 1:1 ratio to cancel 1s. It can be done after rolling the table.
- It is possible to cast without any available [[stat_resolve|Resolve]].
- A result of five 1s (`BRUTAL` [[mechanic_intensity|Intensity]]) automatically kills the caster.

If you don't want to roll in a table, use the 66 result:
- `MINOR` (1 1s): The spell also affects an unintended target.
- `SERIOUS` (2 1s): The spell only affects an unintended target.
- `SEVERE` (3 1s): The spell also affects you, or if you were the target, everyone nearby.
- `CRITICAL` (4 1s): The GM twists the intent of the spell to ruin your day/life.
