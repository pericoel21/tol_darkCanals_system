## Anti-magic
- Lattices cannot be the [[magic_casting#Subject|Subject]] of a spell.
- They can be [[magic_aiming|targeted]], thoug.

### Lattices, traces of Lattices, amorphous materials
#### Those are Lattices
- Metals & Gems are the typical lattice.
- Ordered structures like crystals, computer chips...
- Willpower (see [[magic_spellResistance|Spell Resistance]]).

#### Those aren't Lattices
The following can still be the [[magic_casting#Subject|Subject]] of a spell:
- Stuff with trace amounts of metal (rocks, blood...).
- Obsidian and other glasses, which are unordered.
