# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

- Making a ritual circle uses a whole stick of [[item_chalk|chalk]].
- A ritual circle can hold one spell.
- The spell is [[magic_casting|casted]] as regular.
- The circle holds the spell without any character [[magic_concentration|concentrating]] on the spell.
- The spell can be left prepared or unfinished.
- The [[magic_casting|Casting]] can be a group effort (simultaneous or not).
- The spell can be added to in multiple, non consecutive attempts, as long as the circle is intact.
- The spell can be interrupted on purpose (it fails) by destroying the circle.
- The spell also fails on a failed roll.
- The spell can be unleashed by a [[wizard]], using [[skill_reason|REASON (WITS)]].
- For each 10 minutes of [[magic_casting|casting]], the spell duration on release is increased one hour.

## [[Cursed!]] Characters & Rituals
- A [[Cursed!]] character might cast a spell of any level.
- Other [[wizards]] might pay [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] to reduce [[magic_mishaps|Magic Mishaps]].
- The effects of [[magic_mishaps|Magic Mishaps]] are shared by everyone.
