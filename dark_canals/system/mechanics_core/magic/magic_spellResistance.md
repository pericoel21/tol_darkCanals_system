# `= this.file.aliases[0]`

Resisting involves an [[mechanic_opposedRolls|Opposed Roll]]. Therefore,
- PCs [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] to resist.
- NPCs reduce the [[mechanic_degreesOfSuccess|Degrees of Success]] of a PC's [[magic_casting|Casting roll]].

## Subject
- If the [[magic_casting#Subject|Subject]] of a spell is in the same [[combat_distance|Zone]] as the creature, they can resist with [[skill_insight|INSIGHT]].
- If a creature is the [[magic_casting#Subject|Subject]] of a spell, they can resist it with [[skill_insight|INSIGHT]] or [[skill_endure|ENDURE]].

## Difficulty
### For PC rolls
- [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] is `VERY EASY`.
- A success reduces the intensity of the spell by 1 level.
- Doubles with each [[mechanic_degreesOfSuccess|Degree of Success]].

### Against NPCs
Automatically reduced intensity by their [[mechanic_attributes|Attribute]] + [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] rating.
