## How to Shoot Magic
### Subject
[[magic_casting#Subject|Subject]] to be launched:
- Create it with a spell ([[simultaneous roll|with two simultaneous rolls]]).
- Or, select something already there.
- Must be a more-or-less homogenous mass (like a ball of fire, or a pile of rocks)
- Must be at least `MEDIUM` [[mechanic_size|Size]].

### Roll
> See [[magic_casting#Subject vs. Target|Casting]]

- Use [[technique_transformation|TRANSFORMATION]].
- If [[magic_casting#Subject|Subject]] is `HUGE` in [[mechanic_size|Size]], it affects an entire [[combat_distance|Zone]].
- Can aim at multiple targets & move a specific distance, see table.
