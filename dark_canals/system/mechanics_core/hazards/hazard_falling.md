## Injuries from Falling
When a character falls, an [[mechanic_injury|Injury]] is rolled.
- [[mechanic_injury|Injury Level]] starts at 1 and increases for every 5 ft.
- [[mechanic_injury|Injury's]] [[combat_hitLocations|Hit Location]] is rolled as if aiming for one leg or the head (GM discretion).
- Falling damage ignores metal [[mechanic_armour|Armour]].

## Parkour
If deliberately falling, [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] [[skill_mobility|MOBILITY]].

On a success, the [[mechanic_injury|Injury Level]] is reduced by one level, and the [[combat_hitLocations|Hit Location]] is the leg it affects is choosen by the player.
