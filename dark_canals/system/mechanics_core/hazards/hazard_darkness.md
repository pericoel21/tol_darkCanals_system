## Sight-based Actions
- *Low Light:* [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] for actions that require sight is `HARD`.
- *Pitch Darkness:* [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] for actions that require sight is `VERY HARD`.
- One torch makes things `HARD`, and two, `AVERAGE`.

## Mobility Actions
- Moving more than [[combat_distance|Half a Chain (one Zone)]] requires a [[skill_mobility|MOBILITY]] roll.
- One torch makes this `EASIER`, two negate the requirement for [[skill_mobility|MOBILITY]].
