## The Night Watch
During the night you can:
- Sleep to recover [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] and recover from [[condition_exhaustion|Exhaustion]]
- Keep moving, if you're in a hurry.

If PCs forgo camping or sleeping, they can cover extra distance, at the same rate as [[mechanic_overlandTravel#Travel Distance|Travel Distance]].

At sunrise if a character has not had at least one watch of sleep, they suffer [[condition_exhaustion|Exhaustion]].

### Dogwatches
The night watch is split into two Dogwatches of three hours each.

Someone who spends one dogwatch asleep and one dogwatch as a lookout still avoids [[condition_exhaustion|Exhaustion]], but only recover as much [[stat_stamina|Stamina]] as hours at the normal rate.

To determine when during a watch something happens, just roll a D6, the result is how many hours into the watch it occurs.

### Nightfall & Daybreak
Each day, at the start of the evening watch, and at the start of the morning watch, the GM will indicate that it is Nightfall or Daybreak respectively.
- At Nightfall PCs suffer one level of [[condition_exhaustion|Exhaustion]].
- At Daybreak PCs suffer one level of [[condition_dehydration|Dehydration]] and [[condition_starvation|Starvation]].
- If PCs have been in extreme conditions, they have to pass an [[skill_endure|ENDURE]] [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] or get a level of [[condition_exposure|Exposure]].
- If a PC's weapon was drawn or used in the last day, then at Daybreak the weapon becomes Rusty if it was't oiled.
