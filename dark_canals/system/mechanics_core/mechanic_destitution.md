Living Costs are considered a [[mechanic_coin|Trivial Cost]]. As long as the Character has some [[mechanic_coin|COIN]], they can afford to pay their immediate needs.

## Being Destitute
A Destitute character cannot wave off the [[mechanic_coin|payment of Trivial Cost goods or services]]:
- Sleep on the street.
- Exchange food and lodging for favours.
- Work, if you have a [[profession|Profession]] [[mechanic_profficiencies|Proficiency]].
