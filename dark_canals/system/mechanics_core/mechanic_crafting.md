# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

Professional craftsmen in their workshops can make good quality items. Whatever PCs make isn’t going to be the best quality, so it is not worth [[mechanic_coin|COIN]].

> If you were an excellent craftsman, you wouldn’t be an adventurer, would you?
## Raw Materials
The raw materials for crafting are represented as "Scrap".

Scrap can be just about anything, but the right kind of scrap is needed to make the right kind of item.

## Crafting
Turn the right type of scrap into an item with a [[skill_techne|TECHNE]] [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]].

Succeed or fail, the scrap is used up.

### Difficulty
- Without the tools required, crafting is `IMPOSSIBLE`
- Crafting in a workshop or similar will make things `EASIER`.
- The starting [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] is `VERY HARD`.
- The [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] is made `EASIER` for each additional portion of Scrap used, to a maximum of five Scrap.
- If an item requires more than one kind of Scrap, one of each type is needed to lower the [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] by one step.
