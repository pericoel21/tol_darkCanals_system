# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.

These characters are the people who make the place you call home feel like home, and as such cannot be travelling companions. They’re the people you leave somewhere safe, and return to after a long time away, when you need to recover from the long-term grind of adventuring.

## Corer Contacts in Play
Core Contacts can be called upon to recover from the [[condition_burnout|Burnout Condition]] through [[mechanic_fulfillingResponsibilities|Fulfilling Responsibilities]].

## Creating Core Contacts
During [[character_creation|Character Creation]], each Player creates two NPCs as Core Contacts.

Each Player then picks another Player PC's core contact as one of their own, too. The NPC becomes a mutual contact.

> Players should discuss the extent of that relationship.
