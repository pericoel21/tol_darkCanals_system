## Requirements
In order to Fulfill a Responsibility, a PC must:
- Spend a [[coreMec_watches|Watch]] with one of their Core Contacts.
- Spend one [[stat_resolve|Resolve]].

## Using XP from Fulfilling Responsibilities
Fulfilling a Responsibility grants one [[xp|XP]].
- The [[xp|XP]] from this source is marked as an `X` in the next available tracker's box.

### Shared Core Contact
If another PC joins the activity of Fulflling a Responability with a shared Core Contact:
- The PC who started the activity gains two [[xp|XP]].
- The PC who jouined the activity gets one [[xp|XP]].

### Recovering
[[xp|XP]] obtained through Fulfilling a Responsibility is used to heal [[stat_resolve|Resolve]] lost to [[cond_burnout|Burnout]].

For each level of the [[cond_burnout|Burnout Condition]]:
- 5 XP are required.
- Additionally, the PC must spend a [[coreMec_watches|Watch]] of [[downtime|Downtime]] with their [[coreMec_coreContact|Core Contact]], experiencing closeness and comfort with them.

> Rather than ‘experience’ per se, this is more like a measure of what you've put into your relationships, and spending it is relying on that Core Contact to put you back on an even keel psychologically.
