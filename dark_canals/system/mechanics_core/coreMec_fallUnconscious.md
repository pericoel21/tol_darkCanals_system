A player can choose to make their PC unconscious at any point if:
- The PC has suffered a [[coreMec_injury#Lethal Injuries|Lethal Injury]].
- Or, the PC has suffered [[coreMec_attributeDamage|Attribute Damage]] exceeds their maximum in that [[coreMec_attributes|Attribute]].

While Unconscious, [[skill_endure|ENDURE]] [[coreMec_rollResolution|Rolls]] to avoid [[coreMec_death|Death]] from a [[coreMec_injury|Lethal Injury]] Injury are at `EASY` [[coreMec_difficulty|Difficulty]].
