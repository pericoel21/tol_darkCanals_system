## Effects of a Horse
A single horse can:
- Be ridden.
- Carry full saddlebags.

If they do both, they are `Encumbered`.

### Travel Speed
If travelling `Unencumbered`, a horse adds an additional [[mechanic_overlandTravel|league]] per day to the speed of travel.
- That happens if everyone in the party is able to keep up (so, everyone is riding).
- Only horses with the [[trait_horse_pack|Pack Horse]] [[mechanic_itemTraits|Trait]] get to apply the speed in [[mechanic_overlandTravel|difficult terrain]].

### Carry Capacity
A horse can carry 20 rows of inventory in its saddlebags.
- If `Unincumbered`, the horse still gets to apply the additional [[mechanic_overlandTravel|league]] per day.


## Horse Traits
Each adds 1 [[mechanic_coin|COIN]] to the price of the Horse (minimum of one).
