## Paying for Repairs
Repairing [[mechanic_armour|Armour]] at an armour smith costs 1 [[mechanic_coin|COIN]] for an inventory-worth of pieces of armor.
- [[traits_rusty|Rusty]] armour is irreparable and worthless.

## Doing it Yourself
### Requirements
- [[item_armourerTools|Armourer's Tools]].
- [[item_leatherScrap|Leather Scrap]] or [[item_metalScrap|Metal Scrap]] depending on the type of armour. Scrap gets consumed.
- A forge and [[item_smithingTools|Smithing Tools]] are required to repair [[hub_armour_plate|plate]] and [[hub_armour_reinforcedPlate|reinforced plate]] armour.

### Roll
> See [[mechanic_crafting|Crafting]].

[[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] [[skill_techne|TECHNE (AGILITY)]], `VERY HARD` [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]].
- Each extra [[mechanic_crafting|Scrap]] used lowers the [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] one step `EASIER`.
