## Using Danger Dice
### The Third Color
Danger Die should use a different color than black or white, to separate them from the other two types.

### Assembling the Pool
As regular:
1. Add [[mechanic_skills|Skill]] and [[mechanic_attributes|Attribute]] scores, and the [[mechanic_modifier|Modifier]] if granted.
2. Substitute regular die with [[stat_stress|Stress Die]].

Introduce the Danger Die:
1. Check the feature that gives you Danger Die to see the number of Danger Die (Danger Number).
> [[magic_spellLevel|Spell Level]], [[mechanics_vehicle|Vehicle]] features, [[mechanic_artefact|Artefact]] features...
2. Substitute a number of regular die with Danger Die:
	- It is not necessary to use the full amount of Danger Die given by the Danger number. For each Danger Die less than the full amount, the [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] increases in [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] by one tier.
	- It is possible to roll with more Danger Die than the Danger Number.  For each Danger Die over the Danger Number, the [[mechanic_rollResolution|Roll]] decreases in [[mechanic_difficulty|Difficulty]] by one tier.

> A fun corolary: Stressed out characters will have higher chances of failing a roll that involves Danger Dice, but at the same time, they are more focused and won't incur in that many mishaps.

### Outcome of the roll
Every feature that grants Danger Dice has an associated Mishap Table (either a default one, or a custom one for that feature).
- Each column of the table corresponds to a level of [[mechanic_intensity|Intensity]] (its Severity).
- Each level of Severity equals the number of 1s rolled in the Danger Dice.
- Some times, the fifth column (`Brutal` severity) is not needed because the result is instant character death.

#### 1s in Red Dice vs other dice

| [[mechanic_rollResolution\|Regular dice]] | [[stat_stress\|Stress Dice]]                              | Red dice                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No effect                                 | Cancel highest dice left in the roll, in descending order | Grants a roll in a specific table, severity given by the number of 1s |

#### Mitigating 1s
- Different features might have different ways to mitigate results of 1 in Danger Die.
- The default mechanic is that 1s can be cancelled by spending [[stat_resolve|Resolve]], on a 1:1 basis.
