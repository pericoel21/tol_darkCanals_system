## Vehicle Stats
- _[[mechanic_attributes|Attributes]]:_ Vehicles don't have [[mechanic_attributes|Attributes]]. Rolls are performed used the PC's attributes.
- _[[mechanic_skills|Skills]]:_ Vehicles add dice to specific skills, denoted as with [[mechanic_npcCreation|NPCs]], with (`+`).
	- The extra dice added by the vehicle are always [[mechanics_dangerDice|Danger Dice]].
	- In consequence, vehicles have custom Mishap tables.
- _Traits:_ Vehicles have custom Vehicle Traits.
