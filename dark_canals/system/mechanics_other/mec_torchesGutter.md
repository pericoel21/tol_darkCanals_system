# `= this.file.aliases[0]`
> `= this.description`.


Every half hour, the GM will say "your torches gutter".

Roll a D6:
- On a 1-3 it goes out.
- On a 4-6 it doesn’t.

If a PC is disarmed while holding a torch, it gutters.

There are certain other occurrences while adventuring that may cause torches to gutter, or even go out completely.
